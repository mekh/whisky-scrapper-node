[[Scotch whisky]] is [[Distillation|distilled]] from water, cereals and yeast. It must be matured for three years or more before bottling, and bottled at 40% or more [[alcohol by volume]]. There are five single malt producing regions, namely [[Campbeltown single malts|Campbeltown]], [[Highland single malts|Highland]], [[Islay single malts|Islay]], [[Lowland single malts|Lowland]] and [[Speyside single malt|Speyside]].<ref name="FAQ">{{cite web |title=Scotch Whisky FAQs |url=https://www.scotch-whisky.org.uk/discover-scotch/faqs/ |website=Scotch Whisky Association |access-date=27 October 2025 |language=en}}</ref>

==Currently operating distilleries==

As of June 2026, there are 154 whisky distilleries operating in Scotland.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Facts & Figures |url=https://www.scotch-whisky.org.uk/industry-insights/facts-figures/ |access-date=7 April 2026 |publisher=The Scotch Whisky Association}}</ref>
